Official Community Rules

These rules are designed to keep the CP 24/7 map fair, organized, and enjoyable for all participating armies. Staff are responsible for enforcing them consistently.

Follow official staff decisions. If you disagree with a decision, raise your concern respectfully through the proper staff channels.

1. World Map Rules

  1. Every server must have clear, recognized ownership.
  2. Land ownership must be updated through the approved process.
  3. No instant takeovers. Follow the official invasion and battle process.
  4. Only one battle may take place on a map location at a time.
  5. Announcements must identify the exact server and room or location.
  6. Do not claim land using fake reports or fabricated evidence.

2. Battles & Invasions

  1. All battles must be scheduled and approved through the required process.
  2. Unscheduled battles and invasions are not recognized.
  3. Respect the announced date, time, and location.
  4. Do not spam announcements or publish fake event information.
  5. Provide accurate attendance and event reports for scoring.
  6. No exploiting game mechanics or deliberately disrupting events.
  7. Give participating armies a fair opportunity to attend and compete.

3. Army Conduct

  1. Army leaders are responsible for their members' conduct.
  2. Do not falsify attendance, scores, battle results, or evidence.
  3. Follow the official scoring system and reporting requirements.
  4. Respect other armies, their members, and their leadership.
  5. Report disputes through the appropriate staff channels.

4. Community Standards

  1. Treat community members with respect.
  2. No harassment, threats, targeted abuse, or discriminatory conduct.
  3. Do not impersonate staff or other community members.
  4. Keep discussions and announcements in their appropriate channels.
  5. Do not deliberately disrupt community activities.

5. Staff Decisions & Enforcement

  1. Staff decisions must be based on evidence and these rules.
  2. Staff may investigate suspicious reports and request evidence.
  3. Rule violations may result in event invalidation or point penalties.
  4. Serious or repeated violations may lead to additional action.
  5. Staff decisions may be appealed through the designated process.

6. Scoring Integrity

Army scores must reflect verified events. False attendance, fabricated results, or deliberate score manipulation may result in penalties. Monthly score resets follow the official scoring policy.
