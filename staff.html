Meet the Team

Club Penguin 24/7 relies on staff to maintain the map, support participating armies, verify reports, and keep events fair.

These are role descriptions, not a live staff directory. Names and current appointments will be added when connected to the approved staff data source.

Chain of Command

  1. Owner / Executive Leadership
    Oversees the community, major policies, and final appeals.
  2. Head Officers
    Manage map administration, army registration, land changes, and staff operations within their permissions.
  3. Editor-in-Chief
    Oversees official news reports and newsroom standards.
  4. Other Appointed Staff
    Assist with assigned duties, event verification, and community support.

This is a proposed display order. Adjust it to match your official leadership structure and permissions.

Leadership

Owner / Executive Leadership

Maintains the overall direction of CP 24/7, reviews major disputes, and ensures staff systems operate fairly.

Administration

Head Officers

Administer map ownership, register and remove armies, and oversee approved land changes according to official rules.

Newsroom

Editor-in-Chief

Publishes official news, checks reports for accuracy, and maintains the quality of community announcements.

Staff Standards

Responsibilities of All Staff

  • Remain professional, fair, and respectful.
  • Keep evidence for important decisions and disputed reports.
  • Never misuse permissions for personal advantage.
  • Protect private staff and community information.
  • Follow official rules and document significant actions.
